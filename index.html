IIT Induction Group Project Mobile Application

Wuzy

Every Connection has a Story

Wuzy is an innovative mobile application developed as part of the IIT Induction group project to help people make their digital connections into meaningful real-world connections. It matches users with like-minded people, communities, and verified events based on shared interests. By combining social feeds, event discovery, direct in-app ticketing, community chats, and friend meetups into a single platform, Wuzy ensures you always show up with your crowd.

My Role & Contributions

For this project, I took charge of the UI/UX design and frontend implementation, transforming concepts into clean, user-friendly digital interfaces. Beyond the code and design work, this experience helped me grow through close collaboration, effective communication, and mutual support with my teammates to bring the project to life.

Visit Wuzy Website

The problem

  • Most young adults often skip events or feel awkward attending gatherings because they lack common ground or have no one to go with.
  • Existing platforms rely on endless, passive scrolling or overwhelming crowds rather than genuine connection.
  • Event organizers struggle to connect with genuinely interested crowds and rely on scattered, fragmented channels.
  • Organizers have to juggle separate tools, external websites, and messaging groups just to manage events, track attendees, and sell tickets.

The Solution

  • Connect users directly with like-minded people who share their exact interests and make new friendships.
  • Shift the focus away from screens toward safe, real-world meetups, local events, and shared activities.
  • Provide organizers with intuitive tools to reach highly engaged local audiences who share specific interests.
  • Offer a creator portal and dashboard where hosts can easily create events, manage attendee lists, sell tickets, and build communities in one place.

Core features - Users & Organizers

Interest Matching Social Feed Event Discovery Ticket Purchasing Direct Messaging Gamified Rewards Event Creation Event Attendee List Organiser Dashboard

IIT Induction Project

Wuzy - Social Events Mobile App

Wuzy is an interactive social platform built during the Infoschol Induction Programme at IIT. It helps users discover like-minded peers, build meaningful connections based on shared hobbies and interests, and join local real-world events seamlessly.

Wuzy matches you with like-minded people, communities, and verified events. Key features include a social feed to catch up with friends and plan meetups, interest-based event and community discovery, direct in-app ticketing with passes saved in one place, and messaging through direct and community chats — all in one app.

Why Wuzy: instant trust with a referral-verified platform, relevance over hype with events tailored to your passions, and an all-in-one experience to connect, explore, secure tickets, and meet up. It works in 4 steps: set your vibe, find your match, show up and level up, and flex your journey with badges and shared moments. A Free plan offers 10 daily connections, while Premium (LKR 499/month) gives an ad-free experience, unlimited connections, full guest list access, and interest-matched attendees.
